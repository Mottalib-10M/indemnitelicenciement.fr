What the site is

indemnitelicenciement.fr is an information site with calculators for employees in France: statutory severance pay under the French Labour Code, notice periods, and unemployment benefit (ARE) under the Unédic rules. Its results are estimates for guidance only. They are not legal advice and do not replace the text of your collective agreement, the decision of France Travail or the advice of an employment lawyer.

Personal data and cookies

The site sets no cookies and the publisher records no personal data: the amounts you enter stay in your browser. The only measurement is a technical, anonymous check of page stability with Microsoft Clarity, running cookieless with page content masked. The details are in the privacy policy.
